# DESIGN NOTES

- The product is a ranking instrument, not a decorative tier board or multi-page research site.
- Use one visual hierarchy: rank → model → normalized score → provider/spec context → source evidence.
- External source ranks must be labeled by source and visually subordinate to the site rank.
- Dense data must remain readable through spacing, typography, alignment, contrast, and progressive disclosure rather than adding more cards.
- Color is semantic accent, not the primary carrier of legibility.
- Keep the experience memorable through strong composition and a coherent identity, not copied styling from another BITwiki product.
- References must be directly actionable from the ranking surface and expanded model context.
- Methodology belongs on the same page and should explain normalization without exposing development-history commentary.
