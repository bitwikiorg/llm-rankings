# Provenance Policy

- Prefer exact-model measurements from primary benchmark/provider sources.
- Keep each source on its native published scale in evidence displays.
- Provide direct source URLs wherever available.
- Do not infer an external leaderboard rank from a score unless that source publishes the rank.
- Do not fill missing exact-model evidence with a related family/model value.
- Site-owned normalized ranks must expose their weighting and missing-data policy.
